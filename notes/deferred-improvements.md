# Deferred improvements

- Refined carb share counts legume/vegetable carbs as refined since they lack the whole_grain tag. Consider a carve-out for legume_protein and vegetable role tags in a future tuning pass.
- GL sub-score bucketing means same-bucket swaps (e.g. white rice → basmati at 150g raw) show delta 0. Consider a continuous GL sub-score or finer bucket boundaries in a future tuning pass.
- MVP checklist item "Dal + whole wheat roti + spinach — both scores Low" FAILS: diabetes lands Moderate (42–50) at every realistic portioning. Two causes: dal carbs count as refined (first item above — 10 pts even at 38% share) and GL bucketing on raw-flour carb density (raw atta 35g already puts GL at 20 → 20 pts). CVD is Low as expected. Needs the refined-carb carve-out plus a GL bucket review to pass.
- Step 26 edge case: an 80g spinach-only meal scores Diabetes 60 (High) / CVD 42 (Moderate), not "0 or very low" as expected. Cause: absence-based sub-scores (fiber 1.8g → 20 pts, 0% quality protein → 15 pts, fat-quality fallback ratio 0.8 → 22 pts) plus vegetable carbs counting as refined (25 pts — see first item). Small single-vegetable meals are punished for what they lack. Consider scaling absence penalties by meal energy, or a minimum-meal-size gate, in a future tuning pass. No division-by-zero errors occur (share functions guard totalCarbs/totalProtein/totalSfa === 0).
