# Deferred improvements

## Done in Step 33
- Refined-carb carve-out: `refinedCarbShare` now only counts `starch_source`/`sweetener` ingredients; legume/vegetable/dairy/animal/nut carbs are excluded from both numerator and denominator. Dal-tadka meal now scores diabetes Low (21).
- Continuous `glSubScore` replaced the hard buckets, so GL improvements always produce a delta.

## Done in Step 34
- Absence/quality penalties now scale with load. `refined_carb`, `fiber` and `protein_quality` (diabetes) are multiplied by `carbScale = min(1, carb_g / CARB_QUALITY_REF_G)`; CVD `fat_quality` by `fatScale = min(1, fat_g / FAT_QUALITY_REF_G)` (`CARB_QUALITY_REF_G = 100`, `FAT_QUALITY_REF_G = 30`). `glSubScore`, `sfaSubScore` and `sodiumSubScore` are left unscaled (already portion-based). This resolves the "absence penalties punish small meals" note below: a small plain-starch portion no longer stacks the full refined+fiber+protein penalties on top of its GL. White rice 150g cooked: 89 High → **53 Moderate**; 250g cooked → 75 High (portion-sensitive gradient); dal tadka CVD 45 → 26 Low. Sub-scores are rounded for whole-point breakdown display.
- Band thresholds recalibrated Low <35 / Moderate 35–64 / High ≥65 (was <30 / 30–59 / ≥60), fitting the 8 reference meals.
- NOTE the fix scales by carb/fat load, not the energy-density approach floated below; the hard buckets inside `fiberSubScore`/`proteinQualitySubScore` remain (still a candidate for continuous curves). Known residual: the 8 balanced reference meals span only Low–Moderate — a genuine High needs a genuinely poor meal (large plain refined carb, or high-sfa plate), which is correct; not forced.

## Done in Step 38
- Ingredient database expanded from 39 → 101 records (`ing_040`–`ing_101`) across all major South Asian food groups: additional cereals/millets, pulses/legumes, vegetables, fats/oils, dairy/proteins, and nuts/seeds/high-quantity spices. 13 new GI overrides added to `gi-overrides.json`. Score test (rajma 150g cooked + brown rice 150g cooked + cauliflower 100g) → diabetes 36 Moderate, CVD 3 Low, as expected.
- Skipped 3 requested Batch-2 items (rajma, chole/chickpeas, urad dal) — already present as `ing_034`–`ing_036`; re-adding would have created duplicate records. Added 2 extra staples (amaranth/rajgira flour `ing_100`, sattu `ing_101`) to keep the count ≥ 100.
- Next expansion target: 200+ ingredients. Priority additions: street foods (vada pav, samosa, dosa, idli components), regional dishes (Kerala, Gujarati, Bengali cuisines), packaged Indian foods (Haldiram snacks, biscuit brands).
- Data quirk surfaced during the score test (out of Step 38 scope, not fixed): several whole legumes (`ing_034`–`ing_036`) carry `cooked_conversion_factor: null`, so a "cooked" entry is scored on the raw nutrient profile — inflating GL vs the dal entries (`ing_013`–`ing_016`, factor 2.5). Candidate for a future data pass.

## Still open after Step 33 (deferred to a later tuning pass, per Step 33 scope)
- `refinedCarbSubScore` is effectively binary: `refinedCarbShare` returns 1.0 whenever any non-whole-grain starch is present and 0.0 otherwise (only atta+maida mixes land between). Verified this predates Step 33 — the carve-out fixed *which* ingredients count, not the gradation. `rice + dal + veg` takes the same 25-pt hit as bare rice. Fix: grade by starch dominance (starch carbs ÷ all meal carbs), e.g. `25 × refinedShare × dominance`.
- `fiberSubScore` and `proteinQualitySubScore` still use hard buckets — the same flaw Step 33 removed from GL. A 0.1 g fiber difference (1.9 vs 2.0 g) swings the score 8 pts. Fix: continuous, and scale fiber on density (g per 1000 kcal) not absolute grams. This is the "absence penalties punish small meals" note below.
- Step 33 acceptance checks #5 and #6 FAIL and are left as known gaps:
  - White rice 150g stays High, not Moderate. GL is not the cause (glSub ≈ 30 at cooked portion); the other three sub-scores floor a bare-rice meal at 60 pts (refined 25 + fiber 20 + protein 15). Needs the two fixes above; even then plain rice lands ~75 (High), so a UI "add the rest of your meal" gate for single-starch/protein-less entries is the realistic path to Moderate.
  - Basmati vs white delta is 3–4 pts, not ≥5. The Step 33 GL formula gives glSub 26.0 (GL 22) vs 29.8 (GL 30); the "≈7"/"≥5" targets assumed glSub 22 vs 29, which the specified curve doesn't produce at these GLs. Steepening the 20–40 GL segment to widen the delta would distort every normal mixed meal (they all sit in that band), so defer.
- MVP checklist item "Dal + whole wheat roti + spinach — both scores Low": still Moderate (31, one point over) after Step 33. Refined-carb carve-out now gives it 0 refined pts; the residual comes from meal-level GL (31) mapping to 31 glSub. Fix is the meal-calibrated GL curve prototyped in-session (lands it at ~16 Low).
- Step 26 edge case: an 80g spinach-only meal scores Diabetes 60 (High) / CVD 42 (Moderate), not "0 or very low". Cause: absence-based sub-scores (fiber 1.8g → 20 pts, 0% quality protein → 15 pts, fat-quality fallback ratio 0.8 → 22 pts). The vegetable-carb-as-refined component is now fixed by Step 33. Remaining fix: scale absence penalties by meal energy, or a minimum-meal-size gate. No division-by-zero errors occur (share functions guard totalCarbs/totalProtein/totalSfa === 0).
