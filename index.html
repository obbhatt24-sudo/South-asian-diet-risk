About this tool

This calculator helps South Asians understand how individual meals contribute to type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease risk factors, using thresholds calibrated to South Asian-specific research.

Risk scores are educational, not diagnostic. The formulas are the developer's own design derived from published research parameters — they are not validated clinical instruments. Consult a qualified healthcare provider for personal medical decisions.

Data sources

  • Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB) — ingredient nutrient data
  • ICMR-INDIAB Study — India-context diabetes risk thresholds
  • MASALA Study (UCSF) — US South Asian cardiovascular risk data
  • Augustin et al. 2020 — glycemic index meta-analysis
  • WHO/SEARO 2004 — Asian-specific BMI cutoffs

BMI note

This tool uses Asian-specific BMI cutoffs: overweight ≥23 kg/m², obese ≥25 kg/m². The Western standard uses ≥25 for overweight. South Asians have higher cardiometabolic risk at lower BMI values.
