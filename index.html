About this tool

This calculator helps South Asians understand how individual meals contribute to type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and hypertension risk factors, using thresholds calibrated to South Asian-specific research.

Data sources

  • INDB / ICMR Indian Food Composition Tables — ingredient nutritional data
  • Augustin et al. 2021 International Glycemic Index Tables — GI values
  • ICMR-INDIAB (Anjana et al. 2017) — India-context diabetes risk thresholds
  • MASALA Study (Kanaya & Kandula, UCSF) — US South Asian CVD risk context
  • NHANES 2011–2018 — ML model training data (non-Hispanic Asian subsample)
  • Open Food Facts — packaged product nutritional data (live API)
  • WHO/SEARO Asian BMI cutoffs — overweight ≥23, obese ≥25

ML model

  • Gradient boosting trained on NHANES 2011–2018
  • Features: age, BMI, waist circumference, sedentary hours, 7 dietary features
  • Diabetes: test AUC 0.780, Asian subsample AUC 0.871
  • CVD: test AUC 0.674, Asian subsample AUC 0.781
  • Purpose: personal risk context panel, not meal-level scoring

Tech stack

  • Frontend: Vanilla JS, GitHub Pages
  • Backend: FastAPI on Render (RAG explanations, AI overviews)
  • Database: Supabase (meal history, user auth, ingredient contributions)
  • Embeddings: Voyage AI voyage-3-lite
  • Languages: English, Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu

Disclaimer

This tool is educational only and not a clinical instrument. Scores reflect a single meal, not overall dietary patterns. Consult a qualified healthcare provider for personal health decisions.
